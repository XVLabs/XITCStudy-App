# XITCStudy-App

The X-Introduction To Computing STudy or X-ITC Study for short!

This mobile app is a quiz app designed to aid students with the Introduction To Computing module, teaching them the fundamentals of computing and programming.

It covers eight chapters
1. Intro To Computers
2. Number Systems
3. Hardware Components
4. Operating Systems
5. Intro To Programming
6. Programming Concepts
7. Software & Applicatoins
8. Networks & The Internet

You are also able to review and recheck your scores after each attempt, BUT you don't get to see the right answers. This is an attempt to make students continously try to
understand the concepts and demonstrate them afterwards in exams or other situations that may need this knowledge.
